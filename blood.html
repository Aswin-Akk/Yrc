Our Role in Saving Lives

The YRC Blood Team connects voluntary donors with patients in urgent need of blood. Our volunteers coordinate emergency support and help hospitals find verified donors quickly. The team organizes blood donation camps and awareness programs to encourage voluntary donation. By building an active donor network, we inspire students and faculty to serve the community. Together, we strive to ensure that no call for blood goes unanswered.

Before You Donate

Check these basic requirements before donating blood.

01 🎂

Age & Weight

Donors should be 18–65 years old and weigh at least 45 kg.

02 🩺

Hemoglobin

A minimum hemoglobin level of 12.5 g/dL is required before donation.

03 ❤️

Vital Signs

Blood pressure and pulse should be within the acceptable range for donation.

04 🌿

General Health

Donors should be free from fever, infections and other temporary illnesses.

05 ⏳

Donation Interval

The required waiting period should be completed since the last blood donation.

06 🚫

Temporary Deferrals

Recent surgery, tattoos, piercings or other temporary conditions may require a delay.

✓

Final eligibility is confirmed by the medical team at the time of donation.

BLOOD DONATION

Blood Donation Drive & Smart Registry

Our blood donation drives form the foundation of the YRC Blood Team’s emergency response. Along with encouraging voluntary donation, these drives help build and maintain a verified donor network for future requirements.

✓

Campus-Wide Donor Registration — Collecting verified blood-group and contact details from students, faculty and staff.

✓

Smart Donor Registry — Maintaining an organized donor database based on blood group and availability.

✓

Emergency Donor Matching — Identifying suitable donors quickly during urgent requirements.

✓

Awareness & Outreach — Encouraging the campus community to understand the importance of voluntary blood donation.

02
🩺
HEALTH & WELLNESS

Health Check-Up Camp

Health check-up camps promote preventive healthcare and encourage students and staff to pay attention to their overall well-being.

✓

Basic Health Screening — Checking blood pressure, pulse, BMI and other essential health indicators.

✓

Eye Check-Up — Providing basic vision screening and awareness about eye health.

✓

Health Consultation — Offering guidance on healthy habits, nutrition and preventive care.

03
❤️
VOLUNTARY SERVICE

Voluntary Blood Camp

The YRC Blood Team organizes voluntary blood donation camps with proper medical support and a focus on safe, responsible donation practices.

✓

Pre-Donation Screening — Checking hemoglobin, blood pressure, pulse, weight and basic health conditions.

✓

Medical Guidance — Ensuring donors understand the donation process and safety requirements.

✓

Safe Blood Collection — Conducting collection under trained medical supervision using sterile equipment.

✓

Post-Donation Care — Providing rest, hydration and refreshments after donation.

✓

Donor Registration — Recording verified donor details to strengthen the future donor network.
