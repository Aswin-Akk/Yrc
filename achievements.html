2018–19 · BLOOD DONATION

Award for Highest Blood Donation Camps

Anna University received an Award of Appreciation from the Indian Red Cross Society, Tamil Nadu Branch. The award recognized the university for organizing the highest number of blood donation camps during 2018–19. It was presented on 27 September 2019 and received by Prof. Dr. S. Thanigaiarasu, YRC Coordinator.

Achievement 100+ Blood Donations
Activity Emergency Blood Donor Coordination & Blood Donation Camp
Contribution Voluntary Blood Donation & Community Service
SCRIBE SUPPORT

Scribe Work for TNPSC Examination

YRC MIT volunteers provided scribe support during the TNPSC examination. More than 20 volunteers assisted candidates, including visually impaired candidates. They supported candidates at examination centres throughout the examination. YRC MIT was honoured for this meaningful contribution and service.
