Session Team

Learn. Engage. Inspire.

The Session Team of YRC MIT conducts informative and engaging sessions that encourage students to learn, interact, and develop a stronger sense of social responsibility.

ABOUT THE TEAM

Learning together.
Growing together.

We are the YRC Session Team, a team of student coordinators working under the Youth Red Cross. Our main aim is to conduct interactive, informative, and engaging sessions for our juniors.

Through activities, discussions, games, and awareness sessions, we focus on developing communication, teamwork, leadership, creativity, and confidence among students.

We believe that learning becomes more meaningful when everyone gets a chance to participate, share their ideas, and learn from one another.

We create an experience, inspire participation, and grow together.

OUR SERVICE ACTIVITIES

Spreading Care Through Action

Through meaningful visits, interactive sessions, celebrations, and essential support, the team creates opportunities to connect, learn, and share moments of joy.

01 • ORPHANAGE VISITS

Sharing Time & Care

The team regularly visits orphanages to spend meaningful time with children through conversations, games, activities, and companionship.

These visits create a welcoming environment where volunteers and children can connect, share experiences, and build meaningful relationships.

Companionship Care Connection
02 • INTERACTIVE SESSIONS

Learning Through Interaction

Interactive sessions are conducted to encourage learning, creativity, awareness, and active participation among children.

Volunteers engage children through educational, creative, and skill-based activities that make learning enjoyable and meaningful.

Learn Create Participate
03 • CELEBRATIONS

Creating Moments of Joy

Special occasions and important days are celebrated together to create joyful and memorable experiences for the children.

Through simple celebrations, activities, and shared moments, volunteers bring happiness while strengthening the bond between the YRC team and the children.

Celebrate Share Remember
04 • ESSENTIAL SUPPORT

Supporting Everyday Needs

The team contributes useful materials, resources, and other forms of support based on the needs of the children and the institutions.

Every contribution is made with the aim of providing practical support and making a positive difference in everyday life.

Support Contribute Empower
