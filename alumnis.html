ABOUT THE TEAM

Connecting the Past with the Future

The Alumni Team brings together former YRC volunteers and the current generation of students, creating a strong and lasting community beyond college life.

Through mentorship, knowledge sharing, career guidance, opportunities, and meaningful contributions, our alumni continue to support and inspire today's volunteers.

From supporting students through scholarships to contributing to YouthFest and sharing professional experiences, our alumni remain an important part of the YRC MIT journey.

YOUTHFEST

Celebrating the Spirit of Youth

Where energy meets talent, and generations come together.

YouthFest is one of the signature celebrations of YRC, bringing students together to celebrate, compete, create, and connect.

YRC alumni contribute through financial support, guidance, resources, and encouragement, helping transform ideas into memorable experiences for students.

More than an event, YouthFest reflects the shared spirit of the YRC family, where generations come together to create opportunities and celebrate the enthusiasm of youth.

YRC ALUMNI MEET

Connecting Generations

Memories, conversations, ideas, and new beginnings.

The YRC Alumni Meet brings together alumni and current volunteers for an evening of reflection, interaction, and celebration.

The meet provides an opportunity to look back at the activities and achievements of the academic year, share experiences, and discuss ideas for strengthening future YRC initiatives.

Beyond discussions and planning, it creates space for casual conversations, laughter, memories, and meaningful connections between generations of YRC volunteers.
